# Expanse: Sol at War 0.29.0

This unofficial *The Expanse* fan-mod update is intended for **Sins of a Solar Empire II 2.1.1**. It follows the [0.28 B120 Combined Fleet release](https://github.com/Blean69/The-Expanse-Sins-of-Sol/releases/tag/v0.28-b120-c4). The owner playtested the locally installed 0.29 release candidate and reported that it looked good. Multiplayer and every campaign/scenario path have not been certified.

## New ships and faction content

- The OPA gains the **Belter Skiff** PDC corvette, **Tynan** two-PDC torpedo frigate and **Gatamang** four-PDC escort, with bespoke hull art, rotary guns, HUD presentation, drives and PDC audio. Gatamang is enlarged from its early test design.
- Behemoth becomes a one-per-player OPA support capital with trade, ship production, refit and repair, fleet support, four component slots and a hull-aligned rotating habitat drum. Tycho gains custom station art, shipbuilding, trade traffic and six dedicated station modules.
- The Belt gains spin habitats, mutual-aid and repair tools, asteroid development, procurement/refit systems, dockworker actions and revised salvage. Ceres and Tycho minor fleets use Expanse hulls. A compact *Recovery at Eros* scenario adds Eros, wrecks and loot.
- Combined Fleet Sandbox can build the new OPA ships. UNN can build **Nathan Hale** from its regular capital yard at the start; Hale gains bombardment. **Truman** moves to that yard after its research chain and remains capped at two per faction.

## Combat and balance

- PDCs have 1,000 penetration and 50% more raw damage than the earlier combat pass. Forward coverage on Europa's Bane and arcs on Raptor and Pella were adjusted. Pella's PDC range matches Raptor's; other selected Belter hulls have 15% shorter PDC reach.
- Donnager's 52 torpedoes ripple visibly from two launch collars. Dark Star, Amun-Ra and Gathering Storm fire two quick eight-torpedo waves before a 120-second base reload. Pella cycles its distinct launcher sockets.
- Torpedoes have revised health, speed and interception balance. The final combat pass raises hull and armor by another 10%; MCRN offensive torpedoes gain 10% speed and 15% turn rate, while UNN offensive torpedoes gain 10% speed without the turn increase.
- Gathering Storm regenerates hull and 100 armor per second even in combat. Its railgun cooldown is halved, and its six PDCs gain 15% range and damage. Scirocco's hull and armor are reduced by another 15%, following an earlier 25% reduction.
- Maximum fleet supply is **2,000 MCRN**, **2,600 UNN**, **3,000 OPA**; Combined Fleet remains at 2,000. OPA starting patrol counts were adjusted to fit the starting caps.
- Rocinante has no hull build cost but remains limited to one. Tynan, OPA Expeditionary Command, Dark Star and Raptor cost less; the bespoke MCRN corvette costs 250 metal. Morrigan costs 25% more credits and metal and is capped at 18 for MCRN.

## Fixes and compatibility

- The build updates definitions and references for Sins II 2.1.1, with ship art, material, socket, HUD, Tycho trade/refit, and Behemoth rotation fixes.
- Item rules separate Tycho and starbase modules from normal capital-ship equipment and reserve Behemoth's Port of Last Resort for Behemoth. Torpedo-magazine tooltip health now matches the projectile definitions.

## Known limitations

- Ceres's Free Navy Strike remains experimental: Ceres-owned reward ships may refuse to attack a target allied with Ceres. Tycho's *Beltalowda Answer the Call* reward remains hidden after a prior crash.
- Intermittent PDC target-acquisition reports on Rocinante and pickets remain under investigation. The arc edits above are targeted improvements, not a claim of a fleet-wide fix.
