## Behemoth

OPA gains a logistics titan through Behemoth Logistics Refit, preserving native tier3 titan-unlock costs:1700/300/500,1ultimate exotic,360seconds, prerequisite titan foundry. Construction costs11000/3000/2200 plus the existing Donnager exotic requirement, takes360seconds, uses500supply and shares the ordinary titan cap. It does not create a second titan slot in the sandbox.

Level-one hull12000/armor1500, armor strength55, durability350, speed250 and4degree/s turning. Eight85-DPS Earth-profile PDCs, no rails, and the unchanged Europa eight-round UNN light magazine. No offensive weapon-level bonuses. This is a vulnerable converted generation ship, not a replacement for a screened Donnager in direct combat. Source24046triangles are retained with640small fittings; the2km scale and wide model proportions are explicit design assumptions. All eight measured exhausts and physical PDCs use the compiled model; outer drum bands required conservative outward firing arcs. New portraits depict the actual model. Habitat spin is not animated in this prototype.

Hospital Engineering Teams dispatches a finite800-hull repair budget to one other owned ship within6000:40per second for20seconds,100antimatter,60second cooldown, default repair autocast below80%hull. The existing Scirocco/Fleet Train repair identity prevents additive copies. Station repair reservations and specified native repair effects are excluded. Behemoth sacrifices25%speed and increases normal PDC cooldowns25% for20seconds; scripted torpedo reload timing stays unchanged. Dispatched teams may finish after the provider dies/leaves, but cancel under the inherited target-ownership rule. This is a bounded cast, not a persistent healing aura or interceptable pod.

The native mobile component shop provides refit access without price discounts or global production bonuses. Eight normal titan equipment slots remain. Mobile-shop range/alliance behavior requires observation. Large volume, weak armor, poor movement and the hospital's power tradeoff reflect the show's troubled military conversion and medical use; exact statistics are mod choices.
