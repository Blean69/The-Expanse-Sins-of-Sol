# New hull intake and Foehammer art preparation

This is an **asset preparation report**, not an installed ship release. The Stage 1 candidate must not wait for these later imports. Measurements below come from the actual supplied files; numerical gameplay proposals are mod adaptations. No original asset, installed mod, SDK, or existing ship definition was changed.

## Lore and bounded roles

**Behemoth:** favor an OPA logistical titan: a very large hull pool but modest combat armor, poor acceleration and turning, bounded repair/refit support, emergency medical accommodation, and limited defensive guns. Large interior volume does not imply warship armor. The official TV recap confirms the retrofitted grid overloaded when a missile launch was attempted; another official recap establishes the rotating drum's medical/safe-haven role. These support a logistics-centered design. Avoid transferring the wiki's book gun counts directly into 170 independent game PDC budgets, and do not make it a superior Donnager. Proposed abilities: non-stacking Medical/Engineering Support, finite Ordnance Transfer, and a mutually exclusive low-mobility Anchorage mode. Each depends on verified native mechanics and needs a later measured balance pass. [SYFY: It Reaches Out](https://www.syfy.com/the-expanse/season-3/blogs/episode-recap-it-reaches-out), [SYFY: Fallen World](https://www.syfy.com/the-expanse/season-3/blogs/episode-recap-fallen-world).

**Gathering Storm:** this is a Laconian **Pulsar-class fast-attack destroyer**, not a Magnetar battleship. A scarce late MCRN research branch is the best fit among the three present factions as a disclosed technology-acquisition adaptation; it is not a claim that ordinary Mars built Laconian ships. Start with one per player, a narrow fixed keel railgun, torpedoes and PDC coverage; retain flanking and torpedo-saturation counters. The ship is described as crystalline with pink/blue facets, so a cool pearlescent metallic finish is closer than uniform plain silver. A bounded heat-sink silent-running ability is more appropriate than permanent combat invisibility. No Magnetar field weapon, global attacks, instant regeneration or free conventional shields. Accessible secondary summaries support the ship identity/appearance; exact detailed weapon counts and stealth parameters still require checking the cited novel chapters before implementation. [Gathering Storm reference, citing Persepolis Rising chapter 22](https://expanse.fandom.com/wiki/Gathering_Storm), [Worldcon panel notes, book-8 technology summary](https://seattlein2025.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Bridging-The-Expanse-The-Villains.pdf).

**Murphy:** use as an agile UNN escort rather than another high-armor Truman. The licensed RPG's class is a lightly protected destroyer with forward/aft railguns, two torpedo tubes and PDC coverage; the uploaded model is a fan interpretation and should be labeled accordingly. Give it modest rail damage with narrow arcs, the existing UNN torpedo family and 85-DPS Earth PDC foundation. Preserve a meaningful supply/resource burden so it does not recreate the cheap-gun swarm problem. The publisher preview confirms the Murphy class belongs to the RPG; detailed loadout here is a secondary source citing chapter 4, not a directly inspected complete licensed chapter. [Publisher/distributor preview](https://d1vzi28wh99zvq.cloudfront.net/pdf_previews/328833-sample.pdf), [Murphy reference](https://expanse.fandom.com/wiki/Murphy-class_light_destroyer).

**UN One:** an unarmed diplomatic transport is appropriate. Keep it fragile and provide one bounded local logistics/contact function rather than an empire-wide combat aura or passive free currency. Its short-range shuttle role means a free-roaming phase-capable envoy would be a mod abstraction; a carrier-delivered contact/escort objective would be closer to the depicted purpose. A retailer's manufacturer-attributed product description identifies its diplomatic transport use; this is secondary product copy, not a flight-performance specification. [UN One product description](https://business.walmart.com/ip/The-Expanse-UN-One-Die-Cast-Ship/19526615650).

**Urshanabi:** an eventual UNN flagship can be a labeled reconstruction, but the current geometry is best suited to a wreck objective first. The original environment artist confirms its exterior was deliberately broken and transformed around a playable interior, which explains why reducing triangles or applying a texture cannot repair it. Do not assert an exact canonical class or unseen ventral weapon count. [Original artist's production account](https://tom3d.artstation.com/projects/ndE5ZX).

**IPBM:** the missile is suitable as the visual for **local planetary bombardment**, with damage tied to normal siege pacing. The model alone does not make bombardment destructible/interceptable. Keep global strategic launches gated; the actual-object destruction-cancels-damage acceptance test still applies to any separate interceptable prototype. The model author's archive README associates the design with the opening of TV season 2, episode 3. [Model author/source](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:2639049).

## Foehammer private art prototype

`assets/derived/update20-foehammer/foehammer_orbital_editable.gltf` is an editable, self-contained **10,041-triangle** prototype with a mod-original equipment platform, **one unchanged Donnager Foehammer rail body and two unchanged PDC rigs**. The rail's original local origin, geometry dimensions, UVs and muzzle location are retained. Support geometry sits below the rail with a small intentional pedestal contact; the PDC bases sit at the two outboard arms. No donor gun geometry was stretched.

This is art only: no unit, weapon, research, local cap or build-menu change has been created. The reference 0.19 Foehammer has 5,000 damage, 30-second interval and **zero pitch speed**. A stationary battery's high/low coverage needs an explicit supported mounting/rotation decision; silently giving it full pitch tracking would violate the instruction to preserve gun behavior. Do not call the present mock-up a working orbital defense. Compilation, material conversion, tangent/winding verification after compiler conversion, hull-safe aiming sweeps, firing origins and runtime destruction remain untested.

The prototype JSON records source/dependency SHA-256 values and the exact baseline weapon. Only named regular texture files were copied; no Wine prefix or drive symlink was copied. Existing game assets stay untouched.
