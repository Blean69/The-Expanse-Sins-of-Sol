# 0.19 extracted asset additions — local test use

Supplied originals remain under `/run/media/haker/NVME 2/expanse-extracted/`; source hashes, conversion recipes and limitations are in `audit/update19-europa/` and `audit/update19-haulers/`. Build output is separate. Extracted source assets are not asserted to be open-source or cleared for public redistribution. No remote publication occurs in this milestone.

Europa's Bane: user-supplied `OPA-pirate-ships/meshes/Europa_Bane_Exterior_A.gltf` and adjacent textures/material exports. 100,867 hull triangles plus six articulated PDC assemblies: 104,929 total. Static source PDCs replaced with the established animated turret rig. Four modeled engines; two forward launcher retrofits and a boarding origin. Exact on-screen launcher correspondence and six-PDC loadout are not established canon. Unreal layered materials are approximated in the installed Sins shader; unsupported floating decal cards are omitted. Textured portraits use the final game geometry.

Artemis: full exterior selected instead of the smaller export missing its thruster section. Error-bounded simplification, source UVs/normals and three actual aft engines retained. Civilian tender and automatic trade-hauler roles are gameplay adaptations. Le Guin: existing damaged exterior used for a native recovery wreck, not an intact active hauler. Detailed conversion counts/hashes are authoritative in each integration contract. Manitoba and station assemblies are excluded pending sufficient exterior/assembly evidence.

The official developer describes the game as a prequel in the TV universe with Drummer aboard Artemis: [Deck Nine](https://deckninegames.com/the-expanse-a-telltale-series/). The publisher identifies Artemis as a scavenger ship and its mixed crew: [Telltale, Captain Cox's Files](https://telltale.com/captain-coxs-files-a-cosplay-guide-to-the-expanse-a-telltale-series/), [official Steam announcement](https://telltale.com/the-expanse-a-telltale-series-out-now-on-steam/). Europa's Bane artist attribution: prototype by Tommy Spampinato, final asset by Jonathan Lauer, [artist portfolio](https://tom3d.artstation.com/projects/Z0eblx). Secondary narrative reference for the stolen UNN/pirate identity: [Europa's Bane](https://expanse.fandom.com/wiki/Europa%27s_Bane). Its exact armament count, combat numbers and construction prices here are mod balance choices, not sourced lore specifications.
